The village gate01 / 07

A day of village fun is waiting.

Skip the screens for one long day. Eat at a hosted table, race a bullock cart, row a boat at sunset, and dance around a fire. Scroll to walk in under the welcome arch.

↓ Scroll to start walking

Early morning02 / 07

Chai on a charpai

Your day starts in the clay-walled lane. Sit on a rope bed under a thatched roof, drink wood-fire chai, and eat breakfast cooked by the family who lives here.

  • Hosted breakfast
  • Well-water pulling
  • Clay hut stay
  • Cow milking

Mid morning03 / 07

Get your hands golden

Walk into the standing crop and cut your own bundle. Farmers show you each job, and nobody minds if you get it wrong. Whatever you harvest ends up on your lunch plate.

  • Hand harvest
  • Planting
  • Vegetable picking
  • Farm lunch

Midday04 / 07

The village market

Stalls spill onto the path. Shape a pot on the wheel, taste jaggery sweets, buy vegetables by the heap, and learn to weave a basket from the women who sell them.

  • Pottery wheel
  • Basket weaving
  • Street snacks
  • Local crafts

Afternoon05 / 07

Games everyone plays

Dust flies as a bullock-cart race thunders past. Join a kabaddi match, fly a kite from the rooftop, or just cheer from the fence. Teams are mixed, and so are the ages.

  • Bullock-cart ride
  • Kabaddi
  • Kite flying
  • Tug of war

Golden hour06 / 07

Boats at golden hour

Climb into a wooden boat as the light turns gold. Try fishing with a bamboo rod, listen to the boatmen’s songs, and finish with a picnic on the sandbank.

  • Boat ride
  • Bamboo fishing
  • Sandbank picnic
  • Sunset walk

Night07 / 07

Dance until the stars come out

Dinner is served under lanterns. Then the drums start, the circle forms around the bonfire, and everyone is pulled in to dance. Plan your own day below.
